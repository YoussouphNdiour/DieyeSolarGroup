Solar or diesel generator? The real 10-year comparison in Senegal

Investment, fuel, maintenance, noise, reliability. The full calculation that beats the generator in 95 % of cases.

Why this comparison matters in Senegal

In Senegal, thousands of villas, SMEs and farms run on a diesel generator that kicks in at every Senelec outage — several hours a day sometimes. The fuel bill is 30-60 % of total energy spending. Since 2022, hybrid solar with lithium storage has been able to fully replace the generator for most uses. The 10-year calculation systematically favours solar — even with upfront investment included.

Upfront investment

To power a villa consuming 50 kWh/day during outages:
  • 15 kVA diesel generator with transfer switch: USD 4,200 to 6,700
  • 11 kWc hybrid solar with 30 kWh batteries (DSG Mamelles villa): USD 17,000 to 22,000
The generator looks 3 times cheaper to buy. That's its only advantage.

10-year operating cost

Diesel generator:
  • Fuel: 500-1,500 L/month depending on use, i.e. USD 650 to 2,000/month at Senegalese price (about USD 1.30/L)
  • Oil changes and parts: USD 500-850/year
  • Major repairs: alternator, head, starter — count USD 1,700-3,400 over 10 years
  • Senelec bill alongside: not reduced (the generator only backs up)
10-year total: USD 135,000 to 305,000. Hybrid solar:
  • Electricity: free (sunlight, 15-20 years of production)
  • Maintenance: USD 170-340/year (DSG contract)
  • Inverter replacement in year 8-10: USD 1,700-3,400
  • Remaining Senelec bill: divided by 5-10
10-year total: USD 20,000 to 30,000, upfront investment included.

Net result: USD 100,000 to 270,000 saved

Over 10 years, replacing a diesel generator with a hybrid solar installation saves USD 100,000 to 270,000 at the scale of a high-end villa or small business. For an industry running on diesel 24/7, annual savings can exceed USD 170,000 — the reasoning behind the direct-injection model of DSG's 1 MWc Sandiara plant.

Beyond cost: noise, pollution, reliability

The generator carries hidden costs: noise (90 dB at 7 m for a 15 kVA — unbearable in residential areas), fumes (particulates, NOₓ, sulphur dioxide), vibrations, fire risk (fuel storage), start-up time (5-30 seconds without auto-transfer), fuel supply dependency in rural areas. Hybrid solar is silent, clean, starts instantly (switchover under 20 ms on modern inverters) and depends on no supply chain.

When is keeping a generator still right?

A few minority cases remain:
  • Critical industrial site >100 kW with very uneven load and long-autonomy demand (24 h+): solar remains relevant as the main source, with the generator as the ultimate backup.
  • Temporary mobile site: the generator is still more practical for short runs and frequent moves.
  • One-off needs (party, wedding, event): generator rental is simpler than a dedicated install.
For the vast majority of Senegalese residential and commercial uses, hybrid solar is now the right answer.

Making the switch: where to start

At DSG, we regularly accompany this transition. The approach: energy audit of the site (1 hour on site, free), sizing to real consumption, parallel installation (the generator stays wired during the first months as insurance), then permanent decommissioning of the generator after 6 months of confidence. Frequently asked questions

Does solar really replace the diesel generator?
Yes, in 95 % of cases. A hybrid solar installation with lithium storage powers a site even during Senelec outages, with no noise and no fuel. The remaining 5 % are highly critical industrial sites with long autonomy requirements (24 h+).
How much do you save by replacing your generator with solar in Senegal?
Over 10 years, savings sit between USD 100,000 and 270,000 for a high-end villa or small business, solar investment included. For an industry running 24/7 on diesel, annual savings can exceed USD 170,000.
Is solar as reliable as a diesel generator?
More reliable. A hybrid inverter switches grid to battery in under 20 milliseconds, imperceptible to your equipment. A generator takes 5-30 seconds to start and can fail mid-outage if the starter battery is weak.
Should you keep your generator as backup?
Not in most cases. For residential or SME use, hybrid solar is enough. For a critical industrial site, you may keep the generator as ultimate backup, but usage drops to a few hours a year.
