Lithium vs lead-acid batteries, Deye vs Victron inverters: which gear for your solar installation?

Lithium or lead: the decision that changes everything

Between a USD 700 lead-acid battery and a USD 1,550 LiFePO4 lithium battery of equivalent capacity, the choice looks obvious. It isn't. A lead-acid battery lasts 400 to 800 cycles, i.e. 2-4 years in daily use. A LiFePO4 lithium battery lasts 3,000 to 6,000 cycles, i.e. 10-15 years. Over 10 years, you replace a lead battery 3-4 times, versus zero for lithium. Total cost becomes equivalent — and lithium wins on reliability, depth of discharge (90 % vs 50 %) and footprint.

Dyness, Pylontech, GSB, BYD: which lithium brand?

Brands installed in Senegal fall into three tiers. Premium: BYD, Pylontech, Huawei LUNA2000 — reliable, well supported, global SAV, 10-year warranty. Balanced: Dyness, GSB — great price/quality, positive 3-year field feedback from DSG installs. Entry-level: generic Chinese brands — avoid except for non-critical uses. Our experience: Dyness 10 kWh for villas (good balance), BYD for critical sites (cold rooms, data), GSB for secondary sites.

Deye vs Huawei vs Sungrow vs Victron: the inverter that makes the difference

A solar inverter drives the whole installation: grid/battery switchover, charge regulation, protection. A bad brand ruins a good panel. Deye dominates the Senegalese residential market: hybrid, single or three-phase, 3-20 kW, excellent price/quality, clear interface, local SAV. Huawei shines on commercial and industrial sites: SUN2000 string inverters, native cloud supervision. Sungrow is the industrial pick: our 7 × 115 kW inverters on the 1 MWc Sandiara plant run without fault. Victron remains the gold standard for off-grid and marine: pricey but ultra-reliable and modular.

Panels: Canadian Solar, JA Solar, Longi, Felicity

The three global giants — Canadian Solar, JA Solar, Longi — are widely distributed in Senegal. They are safe choices, warranted 12 years product and 25 years linear production. Monocrystalline PERC and TOPCon dominate, with 20-22 % efficiency. Bifacial panels (Canadian Solar 630 W for example) add 5-15 % production thanks to the rear face catching reflected light — useful on canopies or ground mounts. Felicity Solar is a reliable African brand well suited to local conditions (heat, dust). Avoid unknown brands with opaque warranties: 10-year reliability can't be verified at purchase.

The right reflex: match gear to project

There is no absolute best brand. At DSG, we combine: Deye + Dyness + Canadian Solar for villas (90 % of cases), Huawei + BYD + Longi for industrial sites, Sungrow + bifacial Felicity for large injection plants. The choice depends on 4 criteria: site criticality, budget, SAV availability, future integration (planned extension, supervision).

Warranties: what to check

  • Panels: 12 years product, 25 years linear production (85 % power guaranteed at 25 years).
  • Hybrid inverters: 5 years minimum, extensible to 10 years.
  • Lithium batteries: 10 years or 6,000 cycles, whichever comes first.
  • DSG installation: 2 years labour, wear parts (cables, protections) covered.
Require written warranties on the quote. Serious installers document each piece with its serial number for warranty tracking.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a lithium solar battery last?
A quality LiFePO4 lithium battery (Dyness, BYD, Pylontech) lasts between 10 and 15 years, i.e. 3,000 to 6,000 charge/discharge cycles. It accepts deep discharges (up to 90 %) without damage, unlike lead-acid (50 % maximum).
What is the best solar inverter in Senegal?
There is no universal best. Residential: Deye (hybrid, 3-12 kW, excellent value). Industrial: Sungrow or Huawei. Critical off-grid: Victron. Each brand has its strengths.
Why choose lithium over lead-acid?
Lithium lasts 4-10 times longer, supports deep discharges (90 % vs 50 %), takes 3-4 times less space and accepts higher charge currents. Over 10 years its total cost becomes lower than lead.
What warranty should you require for a solar installation?
Require 12 years product and 25 years linear production on panels, 5-10 years on the inverter, 10 years on lithium batteries, and 2 years minimum on labour. Serial numbers must appear on the commissioning report.
