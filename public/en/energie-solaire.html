Hybrid plant

24 hours with the sun.

How a DSG hybrid plant powers a site from sunrise to sunset, and long after.

  1. 7 am

    Sunrise

    The panels power the site and the surplus charges the batteries.

  2. 1 pm

    Peak production

    The plant covers all consumption: the Senelec grid is no longer used.

  3. 7 pm

    Batteries take over

    Lighting, cooling and equipment run on storage until the next morning.

  4. 24/7

    Power cut?

    Hybrid inverters switch to the batteries without interruption, then back to the grid.
