FAQ

Frequently asked questions about solar, pumping and training in Senegal

The 15 questions that come up most often before a project. Something missing? Write to us — we keep this page updated.

These answers are based on our real projects in Senegal since 2018 — villas, mosques, agri-food factories, farms. For a tailored study, message us on WhatsApp or use the contact page.

Price and budget

How much does an average solar installation cost in Senegal?
A standard 11 kWc villa with lithium storage costs between USD 15,000 and 22,000 all-in. A small 3 kWc residential kit starts around USD 3,000. An industrial 1 MWc direct-injection plant, like Sandiara, sits between USD 920,000 and 1,250,000. Each price depends on panels (Canadian Solar, JA Solar, Longi), batteries (Dyness, BYD, Pylontech), inverters (Deye, Huawei, Sungrow) and site constraints. Details in our 2026 pricing guide.
How much can you save on your Senelec bill?
With Senelec rates around 125 FCFA/kWh (~USD 0.21) and 4.8 kWh produced per kWc per day in Thiès, an 11 kWc villa saves about USD 335/month. A 28 kWc mosque like Abdoulaye Diop (Thiès), which we equipped in 2025, saves USD 1,000/month. ROI sits between 3 and 5 years for a well-sized hybrid installation.
Can you pay in instalments?
Yes. We offer interest-free 3- to 6-instalment plans over 3-6 months: 40 % on order, 40 % on equipment delivery, 20 % at commissioning. We also refer clients to banking partners (BOA, Ecobank, SGBS, CBAO, Banque Agricole) that offer 24-60 month solar loans at 7-12 %/year. For small amounts, Wave is used for the deposit. See our financing guide.

Project timeline

How long between quote and commissioning?
Standard residential or commercial projects: 3-6 weeks after quote signature and deposit. This includes equipment order and transport (1-3 weeks depending on local stock), installation (2-5 days depending on size) and commissioning. For industrial projects >100 kWc, count 2-4 months: detailed study, Senelec requests, civil works, containerised equipment delivery.
How does the site survey work?
The survey is free and takes 30-45 minutes on site. Our technicians check consumption (meter, Senelec bills), measure available area (roof, slab, canopy to build), verify the breaker panel, scout constraints (shade, roof load, access). You get a detailed line-by-line quote within 72 hours, valid 30 days.
Does DSG operate in remote rural areas?
Yes. Many of our solar pumping projects are in rural zones (Keur Madaro, villages across Thiès and Kaolack regions). We deploy teams with the required equipment and logistics, regardless of distance from HQ. For remote sites we also install 100 % off-grid kits (panels + batteries, no Senelec).

Warranty and after-sales

What warranties come with a DSG installation?
Panels: 12 years product and 25 years linear production (85 % power guaranteed at 25 years). Hybrid inverters: 5 years minimum, extensible to 10 years. Lithium batteries: 10 years or 6,000 cycles. DSG labour: 2 years. All warranties are written on the quote, with equipment serial numbers listed on the commissioning report.
What should I do if something breaks?
Call our service line at +221 78 680 31 31 or send a WhatsApp message. Remotely supervised inverters often detect a fault before you do — we call you directly then. Maintenance-contract clients have guaranteed response times of 24-72 hours depending on site criticality. Clients without a contract get on-demand service, billed per time spent.
Does DSG offer a maintenance contract?
Yes, our maintenance contracts start at USD 150/year for a residential install and cover: quarterly panel cleaning (soft rotary brush), annual connection and torque checks, battery testing, inverter alarm review, signed report after every visit. Industrial contracts are tailored case by case.

Equipment and technical

What equipment do you install?
Panels: Canadian Solar, JA Solar, Longi, Felicity Solar (bifacial 630 W for injection, monocrystalline 550-580 W for residential). Inverters: Deye (residential hybrids 3-20 kW), Huawei (industrial string), Sungrow (plants >100 kW), Victron (critical off-grid). Batteries: Dyness 10 kWh (villas), BYD and Pylontech (critical sites), GSB (secondary sites). Selection is project-specific — see our equipment comparison.
How long does a lithium battery last?
A quality LiFePO4 battery (Dyness, BYD, Pylontech) lasts 10-15 years, i.e. 3,000-6,000 charge/discharge cycles. It accepts deep discharges (90 %) without damage, unlike lead-acid which fails at 50 %. Over 10 years, total cost is equivalent to lead — but with zero replacements to manage.
Do you need administrative approval for a solar installation?
For an off-grid site (no Senelec link), none. For self-consumption without grid injection, a simple Senelec declaration is enough — DSG prepares the letter and single-line diagram. For installations that inject surplus into the grid, you need a self-consumption-with-injection contract and a bidirectional Senelec meter. We handle all admin for our clients. See the Senelec guide.

ProSolar Academy training

Who can enrol in ProSolar Academy?
The programme is open to everyone: active technicians, electricians, students, recent graduates, career-switchers, entrepreneurs wanting to launch a solar installation business. No prior degree required — motivation is what counts. 20 seats per cohort.
How much is ProSolar Academy and when is the next session?
The programme costs USD 500 for 6 months (3 months of classes + 3-month internship and certificate preparation). An enrollment fee of USD 170 is payable via Wave on top of the programme cost, to confirm your seat. The next cohort starts 7 December 2026 at ISEP Abdoulaye Ly, Thiès. Enrol online.
What certificate do graduates receive?
The Certificate of Professional Competence (CCP) is issued by ISEP Abdoulaye Ly, Thiès, our official academic partner. It is recognised by Senegalese employers in the energy and electricity sector, and it is a strong asset for landing a job or launching your own installation business. See the solar technician path.
