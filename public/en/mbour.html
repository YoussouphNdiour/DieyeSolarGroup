FAQ

FAQ — Mbour.

Does DSG operate in Saly and across the Petite Côte?
Yes. Our teams cover the whole area: Mbour, Saly, Nguékhokh, Ngaparou, Somone, Pointe Sarène, Joal. For long projects we accommodate technicians locally. After-sales within 24-72 hours depending on contract.
Do you build solar cold rooms for agri-food?
Yes, it is one of our specialties. Our Sandiara installation (two 40-foot containers, 35.5 kWc + 60 kWh) runs uninterrupted. For an SME processor in Mbour or the region, count USD 42,000-92,000 depending on cold room size and autonomy requirement.
