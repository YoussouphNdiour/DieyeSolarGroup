1 MWp solar photovoltaic plant in Sandiara

Sandiara, Thiès region. Grid-tied plant (direct injection) with 7 Sungrow 115 kW inverters, covering 100% of the factory's daily needs.

Capacity
1 MWp
Inverters
7 × Sungrow 115 kW
Connection
Direct injection
Autonomy
100% of daily needs

The need

A factory with high electricity consumption that wanted to control its energy costs and become more self-sufficient.

Our solution

Installation of a 1 MWp solar photovoltaic plant with direct injection, equipped with 7 Sungrow inverters of 115 kW each, designed to cover 100% of the factory's daily energy needs.

The result

This project showcases our expertise in designing and building high-performance industrial solar solutions that optimise energy costs and strengthen our clients' energy independence.
