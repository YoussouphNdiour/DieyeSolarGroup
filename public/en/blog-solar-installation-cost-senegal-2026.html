How much does a solar installation cost in Senegal in 2026?

Real prices by use case: 3 kWc house, 11 kWc villa, 50 kWc business, 1 MWc industrial. Includes cost per kWc, monthly savings and payback periods.

What does a solar installation cost in Senegal?

In 2026, a solar installation in Senegal costs between USD 1,500 for a small home kit and over USD 1.2 million for a 1 MWc industrial plant. The cost per kWc installed falls as power rises, thanks to economies of scale on hardware, cabling and labour. Recent Dieye Solar Group quotes sit within these ranges, outside special cases (complex rooftop, remote site, special imports).

Prices by project type (2026 ranges)

  • 3 kWc home kit + 5 kWh battery: USD 3,000 to 4,200. Covers lighting, fans, TV, a mid-size fridge.
  • 11 kWc villa + 30 kWh storage: USD 15,000 to 22,000. Near-total self-sufficiency, including AC and pool.
  • 28 kWc hybrid plant (mosque-type): USD 37,000 to 47,000. 70 % self-sufficiency, USD 1,000 saved per month on Senelec.
  • 35 kWc agro-industrial site with cold room: USD 58,000 to 75,000, metal canopy included.
  • 1 MWc direct-injection plant: USD 920,000 to 1,250,000 (no storage, 7 Sungrow 115 kW inverters, direct connection).

What drives the price

Four line items explain 80 % of the gaps between quotes in Senegal. Solar panels are 25-35 % of the total: a 630 W bifacial Canadian Solar costs more but produces 8-12 % more than a standard panel. Batteries weigh 30-45 % when installed; a Dyness 10 kWh is twice the price of an equivalent lead-acid battery, but lasts 3-5 times longer. Inverters are 10-15 %: Deye and Sungrow hybrids justify their price with seamless switchover. Finally, labour, cabling and structure weigh 15-25 % — often underestimated by low-end installers, which shows up after 2 years.

How much can you save on your Senelec bill?

With an average Senelec tariff of 125 FCFA (~USD 0.21) per kWh and 4.8 kWh produced per kWc per day in Thiès, the maths are simple. A 3 kWc home produces ~14 kWh/day, i.e. 420 kWh/month and roughly USD 90 saved every month. An 11 kWc villa produces 1,584 kWh/month and saves USD 335/month. The 28 kWc plant at the Abdoulaye Diop mosque in Thiès, installed by DSG in 2025, generates about USD 1,000 in monthly savings.

Return on investment: 3 to 5 years

For a well-sized hybrid installation in Senegal, the ROI sits between 3 and 5 years. Beyond that, production is virtually free for 15-20 years (panel lifespan + inverter replaced once). 100 % self-consumption sites without batteries — such as the 1 MWc Sandiara plant — have even shorter ROI, around 3 years, because the battery investment vanishes.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cost of a solar home kit in Senegal?
A residential 3 kWc kit with 5 kWh of storage costs between USD 3,000 and 4,200 all-in. It powers lighting, fans, TV, a fridge and a few outlets. For air conditioning you need at least 5-6 kWc.
How much does a solar panel cost in Senegal?
A 550-630 W monocrystalline panel costs between USD 90 and 150 depending on brand and technology (bifacial, half-cell). Top brands (Canadian Solar, JA Solar, Longi) sit at the upper end but produce more and last 25 years.
Is solar power profitable in Senegal?
Yes. With average irradiation of 5.5 kWh/m²/day and Senelec rates around 125 FCFA/kWh, payback sits between 3 and 5 years for a well-sized hybrid system, and around 3 years for industrial direct injection.
Does the price include installation and commissioning?
At Dieye Solar Group yes: our quotes include supply, installation, cabling, protections, grounding, commissioning and user training. Every job gets a signed report.
