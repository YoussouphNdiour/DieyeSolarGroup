Abdoulaye Diop Mosque

ZAC, Thiès. Rooftop hybrid plant, 40 kWh of batteries, 70% autonomy.

Capacity

Storage

Autonomy

Savings

The need

A busy mosque with lighting, ventilation and sound systems running at all hours, and an electricity bill that weighed heavily on the management committee.

Our solution

48 bifacial panels on the roof, hybrid inverters and 40 kWh of lithium batteries. The sun covers the day; the batteries take over in the evening and during power cuts.

The result

70% of the energy used is now produced on site, which means around 600,000 FCFA saved every month.
