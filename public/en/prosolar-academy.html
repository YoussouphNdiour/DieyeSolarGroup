ProSolar Academy

Vocational training centre for the installation and maintenance of solar systems, run by Dieye Solar Group in partnership with ISEP Abdoulaye Ly de Thiès.

Why ProSolar Academy

Technicians trained in the field, not just in the classroom.

Solar is growing fast in Senegal, but many installations are still carried out by under-trained technicians: badly sized systems, repeated breakdowns, disappointed customers.

ProSolar Academy passes on the know-how Dieye Solar Group has built on its sites: sizing, installing hybrid inverters, storage, solar pumping and maintenance. The programme is open to anyone who wants a practical trade in a sector that is hiring.

Curriculum

Four modules, from wiring to business.

70% practice and 30% theory: every topic is put into practice on real equipment (panels, hybrid inverters, lithium batteries, MPPT charge controllers, solar pumps).

  1. Module 1

    Applied electricity

    • Electrical basics, direct and alternating current
    • Voltage, current, power and energy
    • Electrical protection and earthing
    • Wiring, connections and reading diagrams
    • Electrical measurements and using a multimeter
  2. Module 2

    Solar system technology

    • How photovoltaics work, panels and their specifications
    • MPPT / PWM charge controllers
    • Batteries and storage systems
    • Off-grid inverters and hybrid systems
    • Installation, configuration and commissioning
  3. Module 3

    Optimal sizing

    • Energy needs analysis
    • PV array, batteries, inverters and protection devices
    • Cables and voltage drop
    • Solar pumping systems
    • Case studies and complete design of an installation
  4. Module 4

    Incubation and entrepreneurship

    • Starting and structuring a solar business
    • Preparing a sales offer and a quote
    • Project management and customer relations
    • Business model and project pitch

Academic partner

In partnership with ISEP Abdoulaye Ly de Thiès.

Dieye Solar Group and ISEP Abdoulaye Ly de Thiès have signed a partnership agreement for vocational training in renewable energy. The curriculum is designed in consultation with the institute.

Classes take place at ISEP Abdoulaye Ly de Thiès. After the 3 months of classes and the 3-month internship, trainees obtain the Certificate of Professional Competence (CCP), issued by ISEP Abdoulaye Ly de Thiès.
