Solar installation in Kaolack

Kaolack and the groundnut basin rely on agriculture and small trade. Both suffer from diesel pumping costs and daily Senelec outages. DSG deploys solar pumping, cold rooms for market gardeners, and residential kits.

Our work in Kaolack

What we deploy in the Kaolack region.

  • Agricultural pumping on Saloum boreholes

    1.5-7.5 kW solar pumps, 40-300 m³/day depending on depth. Typical ROI: 2-4 cropping seasons.

  • Market gardening and orchards in the Nioro area

    Drip irrigation kits + solar pumping + basin, sized per hectare and per crop (30-50 m³/ha/day).

  • Market cold rooms for growers

    10-25 m³ 100 % solar-powered units to store tomatoes, chilies, onions between harvest and sale — zero post-harvest loss.

  • SMEs and shops in Kaolack city

    Hybrid 10-50 kWc plants for bakeries, groceries, workshops, private clinics.

Reference project

Keur Madaro pumping — 2.2 kW, agricultural basin

2026 installation: 2.2 kW submersible solar pump on a 40 m borehole, ground-mounted panels next to the basin. Feeds 2-3 hectares of drip-irrigated market gardening. Transferable across the groundnut basin. See the project.

FAQ

FAQ — Kaolack.

How much does solar pumping cost for 2 hectares of market gardening?
For 2 hectares of drip irrigation, count a 2-2.2 kW system like Keur Madaro, i.e. USD 8,500-12,000 all-in (borehole + pump + drive + panels + structure). ROI is 2-4 seasons — diesel savings repay the investment very quickly.
Can DSG deliver in the Kaolack region?
Yes. Kaolack is 2h30 by road from Thiès — our teams travel there regularly. For multi-site irrigation projects, we organise a bundled campaign, more efficient in cost and lead time.
