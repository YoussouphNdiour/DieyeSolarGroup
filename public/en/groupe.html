About us

A Senegalese group based in Thiès.

Dieye Solar Group brings solar energy, water, electrical work, construction and maintenance under one roof, to support a project from the first site survey to long-term follow-up.

Request a quote

Who we are

Innovation serving your projects.

DSG was born from a simple observation: in Senegal, an energy project almost always involves water, the building’s electrical installation and civil engineering. Rather than multiplying contractors, we brought these trades together in a single team.

Our technicians, electricians and construction teams work together on every site: a 1 MWp plant for a factory, a hybrid plant for a mosque, solar-powered cold rooms for agro-industry, a borehole and pond for a farm, a self-consumption system for a villa.

To train the next generation, the group created ProSolar Academy, its vocational training centre for solar careers, in partnership with ISEP Abdoulaye Ly de Thiès.

Our vision

Clean, reliable and affordable energy for every home, every business and every community in Senegal.

Our mission

Designing well-sized, durable and affordable solar and water solutions, with responsive service after installation.
