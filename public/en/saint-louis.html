Solar installation in Saint-Louis

Our work in Saint-Louis

What we deploy in the Saint-Louis region.

  • Solar cold rooms for artisanal fishing

    10-25 m³ off-grid units + 15-25 kWc + lithium batteries: zero diesel, zero outage, cold chain secured for export.

  • Pumping and irrigation in the Walo and river delta

    2-7.5 kW solar pumps on boreholes, basin or water tower storage, drip or sprinkler irrigation.

  • Hotels and restaurants in the old town

    Discreet 10-30 kWc hybrid systems, integrated without disrupting UNESCO-listed heritage.

  • Secondary homes

    3-8 kWc kits for partial or full autonomy, ideal for houses occupied a few months per year.

FAQ

FAQ — Saint-Louis.

Does DSG travel to Saint-Louis?
Yes. We plan 1-2 tours per month in the Saint-Louis region (site visits, deliveries, commissioning, service). For large projects (cold rooms, big agricultural pumping), we detach teams locally for the chantier duration.
Can you solarise a listed heritage building?
Yes, within heritage rules. We install panels on rear roofs (invisible from the street) or courtyard canopies, with protections required by the heritage commission. We prepare the technical file for the permit application.
